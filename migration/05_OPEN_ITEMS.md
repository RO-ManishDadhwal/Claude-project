## Types

- **`task`** — work described as still to be done.
- **`commitment`** — something the sources record as promised to another person.
- **`deadline`** — a date or time constraint stated in the sources.
- **`follow-up`** — an explicit "come back to this", "check later", "TBD".
- **`open-question`** — a question asked and not answered in the sources.

## Rules

- Due dates are recorded **exactly as stated**, including relative phrasing
  ("end of next week"). Relative dates are not converted to calendar dates
  unless the source itself anchors them; where they can be anchored, both the
  original phrasing and the resolved date are shown, with the anchor named.
- An item's status is taken from the source, not judged. If a later source shows
  the item was completed, the item stays listed with status `completed per
  <source>` and both references, so nothing disappears from the record.
- Deadlines already in the past at the time of migration are still listed. They
  are marked `past-due as of <migration date>`, not dropped.
